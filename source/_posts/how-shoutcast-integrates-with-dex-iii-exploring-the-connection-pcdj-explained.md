## How Shoutcast Integrates with DEX-III: Exploring the Connection – PCDJ Explained

[![Broadcast your mix online with DEX 3 and Shoutcast](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/broadcastmix-shoutcast-1.jpg?resize=845%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/broadcastmix-shoutcast-1.jpg?fit=1030%2C543&ssl=1 "Broadcast your mix online with DEX 3 and Shoutcast")

Shoutcast streaming support was recently added to [DEX 3.17.1](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), but what is it?

The more comprehensive breakdown is from [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoutcast):

> **Shoutcast** (formerly **SHOUTcast**) is a service for [streaming media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Streaming%5Fmedia "Streaming media") over the Internet to [media players](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Media%5Fplayer%5F%28software%29 "Media player (software)"), using its own [cross-platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross-platform "Cross-platform") [proprietary software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proprietary%5Fsoftware "Proprietary software"). It allows [digital audio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital%5Faudio "Digital audio") content, primarily in [MP3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MP3 "MP3") or [High-Efficiency Advanced Audio Coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-Efficiency%5FAdvanced%5FAudio%5FCoding "High-Efficiency Advanced Audio Coding") format. The most common use of Shoutcast is for creating or listening to Internet audio broadcasts; however, there are also video streams.[\[2\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoutcast#cite%5Fnote-2) Shoutcast is available to use for free or as a paid [cloud service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud%5Fservice "Cloud service") with additional professional features.[\[3\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shoutcast#cite%5Fnote-3)

In short, like [Icecast support](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/), which was added to [DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) some time ago, [Shoutcast](https://www.shoutcast.com/) allows DEX 3 DJ’s to easily broadcast their mix show to online listeners.

[![Broadcasting Mix with DEX 3](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pointerfinger.jpg?fit=300%2C242&ssl=1 "Broadcasting Mix with DEX 3")](https://i1.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/pointerfinger.jpg?fit=770%2C620&ssl=1)

For many DJs, Shoutcast is the superior broadcasting option, as it offers a more polished and advanced set of tools, providing a premium broadcasting experience.

[DEX 3](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) DJs that want to broadcast their mix online can [self-host for free](https://www.shoutcast.com/Pricing/Software) or use [Shoutcast Pro](https://www.shoutcast.com/Pricing/ShoutcastPro), which provides a suite of professional features including hosting, monetization of your stream, dedicated radio manager, Simulcast support, and more.

Whether you’re an seasoned pro or new to DJing, streaming your mix online with Shoutcast is not only a great option to help build a following, but also a great way to get in practice sessions to build confidence and hone your mixing skills.

Head over to [Shoutcast.com](https://www.shoutcast.com/) to learn more about options. Once you have your streaming credentials for your online radio show, simply input them into the ‘broadcast’ tab in DEX 3’s options menu. Click the “Record” button at the top of DEX 3 and select “Broadcast Mix” to start the cast.
